01 — Who We Are

A trusted partner for
power and urban development
industry.

Part of Energy China (CEEC) — a Fortune Global 500 enterprise and one of the world's largest engineering and construction groups.

Founded in 1970, China Gezhouba Group Company Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as the "Group") is a multinational enterprise group integrating engineering construction, industrial manufacturing, investment operations and comprehensive services.

As the main contractor of the Three Gorges Project — the world's largest water conservancy and hydropower project — the Group became the first hydropower enterprise listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 1997, and completed the overall listing of its core business assets in 2007 (stock code: 600068).

Today the Group is the only listed company in Hubei Province with an annual revenue exceeding RMB 100 billion, and a constituent sample of the SSE 180 Index, the CSI 200 Index and the China New Infrastructure 50 Index, ranking among China's Top 100 most valuable listed companies.

05 — Kuwait Presence

Twenty-three years
of reliable performance.

CGGC entered the Kuwaiti market in 2003, with the Subiya Water Supply Project for the Ministry of Electricity & Water as its first engagement. Since then the company has delivered four major projects to PAHW, MEW and Kuwait University.

Between 2023 and 2025, three contracts worth a combined USD 1.5 billion were signed with PAHW — making CGGC the largest infrastructure contractor in Kuwait's history and PAHW's largest partner. In 2020 the company registered a 100% wholly-owned WLL entity through KDIPA, and since 2017 has invested heavily in heavy plant and equipment.

Two decades of consistent delivery have established CGGC as a recognised brand in Kuwait's infrastructure sector, supported by long-standing relationships with subcontractors, suppliers, banks, local partners and consultants.

07 — Signature Projects

Landmark engineering, at home
and across the Middle East.

From the world's largest hydropower project to the Gulf's strategic water and solar infrastructure — a record built on scale and certainty.

China · Hydropower

Three Gorges Project

The world's largest water conservancy project — 22.5 GW installed capacity and 88.2 billion kW·h of annual generation. CGGC served as main contractor, creating 112 world-class records, 934 invention patents and 135 quality standards.

China · Thermal Power

Taizhou Power Plant Phase II

Jiangsu — 2 × 1,000 MW units, the world's first successful application of double-reheat ultra-supercritical clean power generation technology at the 1,000 MW scale.

China · Nuclear

Fangchenggang Nuclear Power Plant

Guangxi — Phase I (2 × 1,080 MW) delivered on the Gulf of Tonkin coastline, a benchmark of CGGC's nuclear construction capability.

China · Roads & Bridges

Hubei Hurongxi Expressway

320 km through some of the world's most difficult mountain terrain — the Shuanghekou Bridge's 162 m piers rank among Asia's tallest for curved girder bridges (completed 2009).

China · Water Treatment

Xuanhua Waste Water Treatment Plant

Hebei — operated by Gezhouba Water Affairs (Zhangjiakou), the first centralized sewage treatment facility in Xuanhua District and the largest in Zhangjiakou. A/A/O process with 120,000 m³/day design capacity, Class 1-A effluent and a 30-year TOT concession.

Saudi Arabia · Solar

Al Shuaibah 2.6 GW PV Project

One of the world's largest solar PV plants, developed by ACWA Power. CGGC acted as main EPC contractor; the plant entered operation in 2025.

Qatar · Water

Mega Reservoir PRPSs (Package E)

Doha — reservoirs, pumping stations and pipework for the Abu Nakhla PRPS facility, built as a strategic water storage facility for the World Cup and the largest single drinking-water reservoir in the world.

UAE · Desalination

150MIGD Seawater Desalination Plant

Umm Al Quwain — developed by ACWA Power with a capacity of 680,000 tons per day, the world's second-largest RO seawater desalination project. Total contract value around USD 600 million; commercial operation achieved in 2022.

Saudi Arabia · Urban Development

Saudi Knowledge Economic City

Madinah — CGGC acts as strategic partner under a Framework Agreement covering long-term partnership, financing support and EPC projects, including the Al-Alya'a Residential Project and the IWD Hotel Project.
